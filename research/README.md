
# AeroPod Open Research 🌱

An open research initiative by AeroGreen Asia Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia.

## Our Objective

To investigate scientific methods to accelerate crop growth, improve yield, and increase resource efficiency in indoor hydroponics.

## Research Projects

### EXP-001: Lettuce Growth Optimisation

Investigating how dissolved oxygen and nutrient solution temperature affect lettuce growth in AeroPod's NFT hydroponic system.

**Status:** Planning — Open for collaboration

## Collaborate With Us

We welcome researchers, university students, engineers, and hydroponics enthusiasts.

Please use GitHub Issues or Discussions to propose ideas, share research, or contribute to experiments.


# EXP-001: Root-Zone Oxygen and Temperature Optimisation

**Project:** AeroPod Open Research  
**Organisation:** AeroGreen Asia Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia  
**Status:** Planning — Open for Collaboration  
**Research Lead:** Eric Low

## 1. Research Objective

To investigate whether optimising dissolved oxygen (DO) and nutrient solution temperature can accelerate lettuce growth and improve yield in AeroPod's NFT hydroponic system.

## 2. Background

AeroPod is an AI + IoT indoor hydroponic system using Nutrient Film Technique (NFT).

Current growing conditions:
- Lettuce growing period: 26–35 days after transplanting
- LED lighting: 16 hours daily
- Nutrient solution temperature: 24–28°C
- Dissolved oxygen: Not yet measured
- Average harvest weight: Not yet measured

## 3. Research Questions

1. Does additional aeration improve lettuce growth?
2. Does lower nutrient temperature improve lettuce growth?
3. Do aeration and temperature interact to affect yield?
4. Can improvements be achieved with acceptable energy costs?

## 4. Research Hypotheses

H1: Additional aeration improves lettuce growth when oxygen is limiting.

H2: Controlling nutrient solution temperature improves lettuce growth compared with current conditions.

H3: Combining aeration and temperature control affects growth differently from either treatment alone.

## 5. Proposed Experimental Treatments

| Group | Temperature | Aeration |
|---|---|---|
| A | Current conditions | Standard NFT |
| B | Current conditions | Additional aeration |
| C | Controlled at 22°C | Standard NFT |
| D | Controlled at 22°C | Additional aeration |

These treatments are preliminary and will be refined before experiments begin.

## 6. Measurements

- Dissolved oxygen (mg/L)
- Nutrient solution temperature (°C)
- pH and EC
- Light intensity (PPFD)
- Lettuce fresh and dry weight (g)
- Days after transplanting
- Water consumption (L)
- Electricity consumption (kWh)

## 7. Experimental Design

The study will aim to use at least three independently controlled growing units per treatment.

Lettuce cultivar, nutrient formulation, planting density, lighting and other environmental conditions will be kept comparable.

A detailed protocol, replication plan and statistical analysis method will be finalised before starting the experiment.

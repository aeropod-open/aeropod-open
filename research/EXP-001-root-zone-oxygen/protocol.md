# Experimental Protocol — EXP-001

**Version:** 0.1 — Draft for review
**Status:** Not approved for execution
**Research lead:** Eric Low
**Organisation:** AeroGreen Asia Sdn. Bhd.

## 1. Objective

Determine whether aeration and nutrient solution temperature management improve lettuce growth, yield and resource efficiency in AeroPod NFT hydroponics.

## 2. Phase 0 — Baseline Measurements

Duration: At least 7 days of monitoring, ideally including a full crop cycle.

Record:
- Lettuce cultivar and seed source
- Seedling age and transplant date
- Planting density and channel geometry
- Nutrient solution temperature
- DO concentration (mg/L)
- pH and EC
- Reservoir volume and nutrient formulation
- NFT flow rate and circulation schedule
- PPFD at plant canopy and LED photoperiod
- Air temperature and relative humidity
- Individual harvest fresh weight
- Electricity and water consumption

Measure DO at consistent times and record the probe location. Calibrate instruments following manufacturer instructions.

Do not change multiple growing parameters during baseline collection unless necessary for crop safety.

## 3. Experimental Design — Proposed

A 2 × 2 factorial design:

Factor A: nutrient temperature
- Ambient/current operating condition
- Controlled 22°C target

Factor B: aeration
- Existing NFT circulation
- Existing NFT circulation plus supplemental air pump

Treatments:
T1: Ambient + standard circulation
T2: Ambient + additional aeration
T3: 22°C + standard circulation
T4: 22°C + additional aeration

The 22°C setpoint is provisional and will be reviewed after baseline monitoring and literature review.

## 4. Replication

Target: at least 3 independent NFT reservoirs or independently controlled growing units per treatment (12 units total).

Each unit should contain several plants of the same cultivar.

The reservoir/growing unit is the experimental replicate. Plants sharing a reservoir are subsamples, not independent treatment replicates.

Randomly assign treatments to units and distribute units across positions to minimise lighting and environmental bias.

If 12 independent units are unavailable, conduct a clearly labelled pilot experiment and revise the design before making confirmatory claims.

## 5. Conditions to Control

Keep comparable:
- Lettuce cultivar and seedling age
- Nutrient formulation and target EC/pH
- Light intensity and photoperiod
- Planting density
- NFT flow rate
- Air temperature and humidity
- Reservoir maintenance procedures
- Harvest criteria

Record actual deviations.

## 6. Measurements

Primary endpoint:
- Mean marketable shoot fresh weight per experimental unit at a fixed harvest day, selected before the experiment.

Secondary endpoints:
- Shoot dry biomass
- Root fresh/dry biomass, if feasible
- Days to a predefined marketable weight, if repeated non-destructive measurements are feasible
- Leaf count and canopy size
- DO concentration and nutrient temperature
- Tipburn, root discoloration and other crop-quality observations
- Water use (L/kg marketable harvest)
- Electricity use (kWh/kg marketable harvest)

Measure PPFD at multiple canopy positions. Record nutrient temperature and DO at standardised times; automated logging is preferred.

## 7. Harvest Procedure

Harvest all treatment groups at the same predefined age for the primary endpoint.

Record individual plant ID, unit ID, fresh weight, marketable status and visible defects.

For dry weight, dry samples using a documented validated method until stable mass, recording temperature, duration and equipment.

## 8. Statistical Analysis

Summarise treatment effects using experimental-unit means.

Analyse temperature, aeration and their interaction using a factorial model or two-way ANOVA where assumptions are satisfied.

Check residuals and assumptions. Report effect sizes, confidence intervals and actual p-values when appropriate.

Repeated measurements should be analysed with a method accounting for correlation within units.

Record missing data and exclusions with reasons.

## 9. Equipment

- DO meter with calibration materials
- Water-temperature logger
- pH and EC sensors
- PPFD/PAR meter
- Weighing scale
- Air pumps and air stones
- Independent temperature-control equipment
- Electricity meters
- Water measurement equipment
- Camera for standardised photographs

## 10. Safety and Quality Control

- Protect electrical equipment from water.
- Use safe electrical isolation and suitable waterproof connections.
- Follow nutrient chemical handling instructions.
- Clean and calibrate instruments regularly.
- Avoid cross-contamination between treatment reservoirs.
- Document failures and corrective actions.

## 11. Data Management

Use unique IDs for experiments, reservoirs and plants.

Preserve original sensor exports and raw measurements. Do not overwrite raw data.

Record equipment calibration, protocol changes and reasons for changes.

Maintain backups. Publish cleaned, non-confidential data and analysis scripts after review.

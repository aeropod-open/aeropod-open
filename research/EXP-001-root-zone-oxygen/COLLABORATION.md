# Collaborate on AeroPod Open Research

**Project:** EXP-001 — Root-Zone Oxygen and Temperature Optimisation
**Organisation:** AeroGreen Asia Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia
**Status:** Open for research discussions and proposed contributions

## Our Mission

AeroPod Open Research aims to improve indoor hydroponic crop growth through reproducible scientific experiments, open collaboration and practical engineering.

Our first project investigates dissolved oxygen, nutrient temperature and lettuce productivity in NFT hydroponics.

## Who Can Participate?

We welcome:
- University researchers and lecturers
- Undergraduate and postgraduate students
- Plant scientists and horticulturists
- IoT and electronics engineers
- Data scientists and statisticians
- Independent hydroponics researchers and growers

Contributors do not need to be affiliated with a university.

## Research Areas

### Plant Science
Review plant physiology, lettuce growth, root-zone conditions and crop quality.

### Experimental Design
Help improve treatment selection, replication, randomisation and statistical validity.

### Engineering and IoT
Develop sensor calibration methods, data collection tools and environmental control approaches.

### Data Analysis
Contribute reproducible Python or R scripts, visualisations and statistical methods.

### Scientific Writing
Assist with literature reviews, manuscript structure and interpretation of findings.

## How to Join

1. Read the project README and protocol.
2. Open a GitHub Discussion to introduce yourself and your interests.
3. Open an Issue to propose a research question or contribution.
4. Agree on the scope of work with the project maintainers.
5. Submit documentation, code or analysis through a Pull Request.

Please avoid submitting confidential data or third-party copyrighted materials without permission.

## Suggested First Contributions

- Review three scientific papers on DO and lettuce growth.
- Recommend a reliable low-cost DO measurement method.
- Review the proposed experimental design for confounding factors.
- Develop a standard crop measurement spreadsheet.
- Propose statistical analysis methods for factorial experiments.
- Review energy and water-use efficiency calculations.

## Research Integrity

All contributors are expected to:
- Report observations and findings accurately.
- Distinguish hypotheses from experimental results.
- Preserve original data and document changes.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect participant privacy and research agreements.
- Avoid fabrication, falsification and plagiarism.

## Authorship and Recognition

Contributions will be acknowledged appropriately.

Authorship of a scientific paper is not automatic and will be determined by substantive scholarly contributions and the target journal's authorship policies.

Authors should participate in reviewing and approving the final manuscript and accept appropriate responsibility for their contributions.

Code and documentation contributors may also be recognised in the repository.

## Intellectual Property and Licensing

AeroPod Open Research is associated with a commercial technology platform.

Public contributions are subject to the repository's applicable licence and contribution policies. Before substantial collaborative work, the parties should agree in writing on ownership, licensing, confidentiality, publication rights and any potentially patentable results.

Do not submit confidential or proprietary material without authorisation.

## Data Sharing

Approved non-confidential datasets, protocols and analysis scripts may be made publicly available.

Raw data will be preserved, and published datasets should include metadata, units, experimental conditions and version information.

## University Collaboration

Formal university collaborations may require separate agreements covering research responsibilities, facilities, funding, IP and publication rights.

# AeroPod Open 🌱

**Open-source AI + IoT precision farming for education, research and sustainable urban agriculture.**

AeroPod Open is an initiative by **AeroGreen Asia Sdn Bhd, Malaysia**.

## Our Vision

To make precision farming technology more accessible to students, educators, researchers, developers and growers.

We want AeroPod Open to become a platform where the community can learn, experiment and build new applications around AI, IoT and hydroponics.

## What We Aim to Build

AeroPod Open will explore:

- Open APIs for precision-farming data
- IoT sensor integrations
- pH, EC and water-temperature monitoring
- ESP32 / Arduino integrations
- Hydroponic automation
- Water usage monitoring
- Lighting and environmental control
- AI and data-analysis experiments
- Student and university research projects
- Third-party hardware integrations

## Who Can Participate?

We welcome:

- Software developers
- IoT and embedded developers
- AI developers
- Students
- Educators
- Researchers
- Hydroponics enthusiasts
- UI/UX designers
- Documentation contributors

You do not need to be a programmer to contribute.
